Why book an apartment here instead of a hotel room

Same money, more room, and a kitchen. We only list places we have stayed in ourselves.

01

The whole place, not a corridor

Every listing is booked whole — no shared lobbies, no wristbands, no neighbours through the wall at 2am.

02

A kitchen you would actually use

Sharp knives, a full spice shelf, coffee for the first two mornings. Breakfast stops being a queue.

03

Hosts who answer

Every host lives within twenty minutes and replies inside two hours. Gate codes arrive before you ask.

04

Pay the way you pay

Card, a virtual account number, or Midtrans — priced the same either way, with no booking fee bolted on.

How it goes

Three screens between you and a weekend away

1 Filter to your kind of place. Apartment, hotel, villa or home — then city, price, beds and rating.

2 Pick your dates and pay. Card, generated virtual account, or Midtrans. Confirmation lands before you close the tab.

3 Turn up. Directions, gate code and the host's number sit in your booking, editable until seven days out.
